# Design principles

Summary of the rules every Sphere component follows. The full text is in
`docs/specs/20261112-desktop-design-system.md`.

## Controls look pressable without hover

A desktop control shows it is clickable by how it looks at rest. Bordered
controls have a light body and a hairline border; borderless glyph buttons use
the secondary label colour. Press changes colour only, and a toggled-on control
is visible at rest. Disabled controls drop to the disabled opacity.

## Hover

No control has a hover effect. Only what reacts on macOS reacts here: the
highlighted item in an open menu follows the pointer, resize handles show a
resize cursor, and tooltips appear after a delay.

## Cursor

`cursor: default` on every control, row and label, `text` in editable text, and
resize cursors on handles. Never `pointer`, never `not-allowed`.

## Corners

Radii are small and depend on the element: square for fields, tables and
panels, 4px for buttons, 3px for tooltips and badges, 5px for menus and
popovers, 6px for dialogs. Selection highlights have no radius.

## Density and type

Compact density on a 4px grid with the system font stack at desktop sizes
(10 to 13px). Controls have fixed heights: 22px for `md`, 18px for `sm`.

## Selection

Lists and trees use source-list selection: the selected row is filled edge to
edge and its text turns `on-accent` while the list has focus, then falls back to
an inactive fill. Explicitly coloured glyphs keep their colour. No hover tint.

## Focus

Keyboard focus draws a 3px `focus-ring` glow that follows the control radius.
Text fields show it whenever focused.

## Motion

Menus and tooltips appear instantly and fade out quickly. Dialogs fade in and
out with no scale or translate. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns transitions off.

## Popups and windows

Menus, dialogs and tooltips share the popup surface: hairline border, popup
shadow, small radius. Dialogs have no scrim; the context menu opens at the
pointer.

## Colour

Neutral greys, one accent, semantic status colours. Colour is for state and
data, not decoration. The statusbar and footers follow the theme.
